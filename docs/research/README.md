# Design research

- `contact-silencio.png`: twelve frames of silencio.network (October 2026), the reference for the
  brand. It uses a true-black ground, white Geist type, large plain headlines, white pill buttons,
  thin-outlined media and amber used sparingly.
- `contact-redesign.png`: the calendar and stats screens after the redesign.

Direction: *true-black flat*, from the taste-skill app-screen library. Elevation comes from a
lightness step alone (#000 ground, #111 surface), with no borders or shadows on cards. The primary
control is a white pill. Colour is kept for data: here, Silencio's amber marks what waits on a
decision. The type is Geist instead of the style's Inter, because Geist is the brand face.

The layout drops the side panel and the accent-striped cards. The selected day's week opens into a
full-width sheet below it, the cell and the sheet share one surface like a tab and its page, and
posts sit straight on that sheet in the layout each network gives them.
